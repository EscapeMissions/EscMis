Multiple Purposes

Family Worship
Home, sweet home: the mission of parenthood.
Family is where faith takes root and grows strong. Parents are called not only to feed their children with food but also to nourish their souls with God’s Word. Reading Scripture together is one of the most beautiful family habits. I love doing it every day with mine.
If you want to make that time fun and interactive, helping your children memorize Scripture while discovering God’s creation, try the Escape Missions book series. Each book unfolds like an exciting escape-room adventure that guides your family deeper into God’s Word and the wonders of His world.

Children’s Ministry Add-On
The Escape Missions book series is a perfect complement to any children’s ministry curriculum. Church case studies show how children aged 8 to 12 embraced it with joy and looked forward to every session. This Scripture-based resource combines faith, fun, and learning in one unforgettable experience.
If you are a children’s ministry pastor or leader, give it a try. Your group will embark on a creation-care adventure filled with wonder, teamwork, and spiritual growth.

Youth/Student Ministry Add-On
Teens aged 11 to 18 are full of energy, curiosity, and questions. They do not need long lectures; they need connection, challenge, and conversation. The Escape Missions book series transforms learning into an interactive experience that sparks excitement and opens doors to deeper discussions about faith, purpose, and creation care.
It is not just another group activity. It is a catalyst for genuine engagement, an immersive escape-room adventure, and interactive fun learning.

Intergenerational Activity
All ages are welcome!
The Escape Missions book series is designed to unite children, youth, adults, and seniors in God’s Word. Each book includes age-specific discussion questions so everyone can participate, from curious kids to wise grandparents.
It is an opportunity to play, learn, and grow together as one church family. Don’t forget to visit worksheets webpage for free, theme-based worksheets designed especially for younger children.

Creation Care
Stewardship of God’s creation is biblical.
Climate change is one of the greatest challenges of our time. While developed nations cause most of the damage, low-income countries often bear the heaviest burden, facing droughts, heat stress, floods, and famine.
As followers of Christ, we are called to respond with compassion, just like the Good Samaritan. The Escape Missions book series helps churches teach and live out creation care, reminding us that protecting the environment is part of loving our neighbour.
