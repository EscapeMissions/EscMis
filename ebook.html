Display Settings in Google Play Books:
1️⃣ After opening the eBook, select Display options.
2️⃣ Enable Dark theme and turn Vertical scrolling off.
3️⃣ Under Page layout, choose One-page layout.
4️⃣ Tap the Full screen button for an immersive view.
Note: In the Zoom menu, select Fit to screen or Fit to width depending on your screen size and what feels most comfortable for reading.
